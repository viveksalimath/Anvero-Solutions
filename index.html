Data Governance

ANVERO Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

At ANVERO Operations Inc. (“ANVERO”), we take client data privacy, customer records, and confidential business workflows with the highest degree of enterprise seriousness.

1. Zero Local Data Storage Policy

All operations specialists and call agents work strictly inside secure cloud Virtual Desktop (VDI) environments. Local disk storage, screenshotting, clipboard copying, and file downloads are permanently disabled.

2. Information We Collect

When you request a Free Operations Assessment, we collect contact details (name, email, company, telephone, interaction volume) solely to evaluate your requirements and deliver your tailored diagnostic blueprint. We do not sell or lease client information to third parties.

3. Non-Disclosure & IP Protection

Every engagement is governed by bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). All workflows, scripts, training data, SOP documents, and customer tickets remain 100% your proprietary property.

4. Regulatory Compliance

We maintain full compliance with PCI-DSS standards for transactional data and HIPAA protocols for medical appointment and patient intake workflows.
